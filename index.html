Privacy policy

Privacy commitment

GymRoulette is ethical, sovereign software: it does what you ask and nothing else.

  • 100% offline

    It works without a connection. The app does not even request Internet permission.

  • No trackers

    No analytics, no tracking libraries and no profiles.

  • No ads

    No ads and no ad networks.

  • Your data is yours

    Everything you create stays on your device. If you delete the app or its data, no trace is left in the cloud.

  • Zero external scripts

    All the code is inside the app. Nothing is loaded from other servers.

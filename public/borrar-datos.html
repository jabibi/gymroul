Data Deletion - GymRoulette

Last updated: October 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of the data associated with the GymRoulette app (Android, eus.jabibi.gymroul, and its web version), developed by Javier Melendez ("jabibi"). GymRoulette has no user accounts: no name, email or password is stored.

1. Data stored on your device

Your exercise and rep settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to our servers. To delete them:

  1. Android: Settings > Apps > GymRoulette > Storage > Clear data, or uninstall the app.
  2. Web: clear the site data for gymroul.web.app in your browser settings.

This also removes your Google Analytics choice and resets the app's anonymous identifier, so later usage data is not linked to earlier data.

2. Analytics data (Google Analytics)

If you accepted Google Analytics, Google may keep usage data associated with an anonymous identifier of your installation. To request its deletion:

  1. Write to javimelex@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion - GymRoulette".
  2. State your device model, Android version and the approximate dates you used the app.
  3. We will reply within one month.

Because the identifier is anonymous and not linked to your identity, in some cases it may not be possible to locate your specific data. In that case, the data is deleted automatically when the retention period shown below ends.

Installation identifier, device data, approximate location and usage events (Google Analytics)Deleted on your request when they can be located; otherwise deleted automaticallyReply within one month; 14-month maximum retention

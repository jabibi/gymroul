Help me test GymRoulette!

Follow these steps to join the 14-day closed testing program on Google Play and help us publish the app.

1

Join the testers group

To get access to the download, you first need to join our Google group. Make sure you do it with the Google account you normally use on your phone.

👉 Go to the Google Group

On the group page, click the “Join group” button, next to the group name at the top. It looks like this (the label may vary slightly depending on your Google language):

Join group

2

Download and install the app

Once you are in the group, use the same Google account to access the store and download GymRoulette on your Android device.

📱 Download on Google Play

Tip: the first time you open the app, a Google Analytics screen asks for your permission. Choose “Accept” or “Decline”: the app works either way.

3

Try it for 14 days

Google requires thorough testing for new developer accounts. It is essential that you keep the app installed and use it on and off for at least 14 consecutive days.

4

Give us your feedback and report bugs

The goal of this phase is to find bugs and improve. Share your experience and tell us what works well and what doesn't:

  • Leaving your comments and rating directly on Google Play.
  • Sending an email to: javimelex@gmail.com.

Thank you so much for your time and help!

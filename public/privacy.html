Privacy Policy - GymRoulette

Last updated: October 2026

This policy describes what data the GymRoulette app (Android, identifier eus.jabibi.gymroul, and its web version at gymroul.web.app) processes and how it is used.

1. Data controller

Controller: Javier Melendez (independent developer, "jabibi").
Contact: javimelex@gmail.com

2. Data we process

2.1 Data that stays on your device

Your wheel settings (the exercises and reps you create or turn on) are stored only in your device's local storage (localStorage). The session history exists only in memory and is lost when you close the app. This data is not sent to any server, and you remove it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

2.2 Analytics data (Google Analytics)

The app uses Google Analytics, a service of Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC, to understand in aggregate how the app is used and to improve it. Google Analytics works through Firebase, the Google platform that also hosts the web version of the app. This service can automatically collect:

DataPurpose
App instance / client identifier (a pseudonymous identifier generated by Firebase and stored on the device)Tell unique users and sessions apart without identifying you personally
Device and system information (model, operating system version, language, app version)Compatibility and statistics
Approximate location (country/region) derived from the IP address; Google does not keep the full IPAggregated geographic statistics
Usage events (first open, usage session start, screens viewed, interactions)Usage analysis and product improvement

GymRoulette does not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, precise location, or any health or fitness data. The app has no user accounts.

3. Legal basis

Processing of analytics data is based on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and Article 22(2) of the Spanish LSSI). You are asked for it the first time you open the app. It is optional: you can decline and still use the whole app, and you can change or withdraw your choice at any time from Settings > Analytics preferences. If you decline, Google Analytics is not loaded and no analytics data is collected. Local storage of your settings is necessary for the app to work.

4. Recipients and transfers

Analytics data is processed by Google as a data processor. Google may transfer data outside the European Economic Area (for example to the United States) under the safeguards provided by law (standard contractual clauses and/or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework). More information at firebase.google.com/support/privacy and policies.google.com/privacy.

We do not sell or share data with advertising networks, and we do not show ads.

5. Retention

Analytics data is kept for the retention period configured in Firebase (at most 14 months) and then deleted automatically. Local data is kept until you delete it.

6. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability, and withdraw your consent, by writing to the contact address above. Instructions for deleting your data are at borrar-datos.html. If you believe your rights have not been respected, you can lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in Spain, the Spanish Data Protection Agency: www.aepd.es).

Because analytics data is tied to a pseudonymous device identifier, to handle a request we may ask you for that identifier. You can also reset it by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

7. Device permissions

The app requests only two permissions: Internet access (needed for analytics) and vibration (for the wheel's effects). Both are normal permissions that do not require your confirmation, and no information is collected through them. The device's audio is used only to play the wheel's sounds.

8. Children

GymRoulette is not directed at children under 14 and does not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

Analytics data is transmitted encrypted (HTTPS) to Google's servers. Local data is protected by the operating system's app isolation.

10. Changes to this policy

If the policy changes, we will publish the new version at this same address and update the date shown at the top.
